## Report a win

1. Open **Captain Dashboard → Score Reporting**, then find the opponent under **Active matches**.
2. If your team won the current game, attach **One scoresheet (required)** (PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF, up to **10 MB**) and select **Report Win**.
3. Look for **Game 1 · reported** (or the current game number). The report is waiting for the other captain, so the confirmed score has **not** changed yet.

## Confirm or dispute the opponent's report

1. Open the opponent's **View Game [number] Scoresheet** link.
2. If correct, select **Confirm Result**. No second upload is required.
3. If wrong, enter a **Dispute reason** and select **Dispute Result**. An organiser reviews disputes before the series can continue.

## Finish the match

Each game winner submits one report; the opposing captain confirms it. A **Best of 3 (BO3)** match finishes at **2–0** or **2–1** confirmed wins. A **Best of 1 (BO1)** match finishes after one confirmed game. Do not report an extra game after the series ends.

Check **Match History** for the completed match and bracket advancement. If you cannot find your match, check your selected tournament and the team linked to your Discord account.
